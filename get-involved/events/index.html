First Annual

Brother's Place Arts and Talents Showcase

A family-friendly night of music, comedy, and talent from across our community. Bring the whole family, enjoy the show, and help keep the doors of Brother's Place open for men rebuilding their lives.

Show Night Saturday, October 10, 2026 at 6:30 PM. Soundcheck begins at 4:00 PM.
Location West Rome Baptist's Warehouse, Rome, GA
Auditions Open now through September 5. Submit an interest form to audition.
Everyone's Invited A family-friendly evening, open to the whole community. Auditions are for performers 13 and up.

Who can audition?

Auditions are open to anyone 13 or older who can sing, rap, play an instrument, make people laugh, or share a talent of their own. Keep your act family-friendly: clean, positive, and suitable for an audience of all ages. Performances that honor God through the talent you share are right at home here.

Selected performers are asked to raise a minimum of $250 through fundraising as their entry contribution. Every dollar stays in Rome and supports the men of Brother's Place.
