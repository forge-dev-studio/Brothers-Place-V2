Become a Sponsor.

Put your business behind Rome's Got Talent on Saturday, October 10, and behind the men rebuilding their lives here in Rome.

Become a Sponsor See the Levels

Your Name, In Front of Rome

Rome's Got Talent brings families from across the community into one room for a night of music, comedy, and local talent. Sponsoring it puts your business in front of that room, in the newsletter that goes out afterward, and on the wall at Brother's Place.

It also does something plainer. Every dollar stays in Rome and pays for beds, meals, transportation, and the mentoring that helps men get back on their feet. Sponsorship is how a local business funds that work and gets to stand next to it in public.

Become a Sponsor

Brother's Place is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. You will receive a receipt for your sponsorship.

What Your Sponsorship Buys

The banner at the show Builder and Partner sponsors have their logo printed on the banner that hangs at Rome's Got Talent. Partner logos also go up on the wall at Brother's Place.
Social media and the newsletter Every sponsor is thanked by name on our social media. Builder and Partner sponsors are also thanked in the newsletter that goes out to our supporters.
A plaque for your wall Builder sponsors receive a plaque marking their support of the first annual Rome's Got Talent. Partner sponsors receive a personalized one.
A table at the show Partner sponsors get a table on the night for their team, their family, or their guests. Bring who you want to bring.

How It Works

1. Choose your level

Pick Supporter, Builder, or Partner on our sponsorship page and complete your sponsorship there.

2. Email us your logo

Send your logo to maloy@brothersplace.org so we can get it onto the banner and into our posts. A PNG with a transparent background or an SVG works best, and please send it at least 800 pixels wide so it prints cleanly.

To appear on the banner at the show, send your logo before October 1. Sponsorships are welcome after that date, and every other benefit still applies, but the banner has to go to print.

3. We take it from there

We will confirm we have what we need and let you know when your posts go up. If you have questions before you commit, call or email us and we will walk you through it.

Talk to us first Call 706.509.9020 or email maloy@brothersplace.org. Maloy Owen, our executive director, handles sponsorships personally.
